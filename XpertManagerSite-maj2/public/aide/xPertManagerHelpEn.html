📋Product description

xPertManager is an integrated management platform that streamlines how organizations operate. Among other things, it provides:

  • management of human, material and financial resources
  • attendance tracking by scanning a QR code with the agents' phones
  • project management: design, planning/scheduling, monitoring and organization of activities
  • event management: creation, planning and sharing
  • communication management: audio calls, text messages, instant messaging, video conferencing, with a notification center
  • organization of electronic votes
  • organization tools (dashboard with Eisenhower matrix, sticky notes, reminders, etc.)

When the application starts, the user can choose the working language: French or English (support for other languages is planned for future versions).

The login window offers three buttons: Connect opens your workspace, New project starts the creation of a new structure and its database (see Create a structure), and Cancel quits the application.

The client interface has five tabs:

General tab

General configuration interface for the organization:

  • creation of structures and sub-structures
  • creation of agents and their assignment to structures
  • creation and assignment of material resources (to a structure and to an agent)
  • attendance tracking (QR code clock-in)
  • communication between agents and notification center 🔔

Dashboard tab

Helps users organize themselves better and see the priority of their pending tasks.

Activities tab

  • creating projects or activities
  • sharing and processing projects or activities
  • monitoring projects or activities

Event tab

  • creating events
  • monitoring events
  • launching events such as video conferences held on the platform

Finance/Accounting tab

Opens the Finance and accounting hub, organized into three groups of modules, each opening in its own window:

  • Sales and purchasing: Customers, Suppliers, Orders, Invoices
  • Catalog and stock: Products, Stock
  • Finance and payroll: Treasury, Bank reconciliation, Payroll settlement

🏗️Creating and organizing structures

Create a structure

  1. When the application starts, in the login window, choose the working language and click the New project button.
  2. In the dialog box that appears, enter the name of the structure and the name of its associated database. Also define the staff categories and activity types by clicking the corresponding buttons. You can also fill in the structure description field.
  3. Then click the Create button.

Add a sub-structure

To add a sub-structure to an existing structure:

  1. Select the parent structure.
  2. Click the button in the General tab.
  3. In the dialog box, enter the required data. Selecting the manager of the new structure is optional and can be done later.
  4. Click the Create button.
  5. You can continue creating other structures or close the dialog box.

Edit / Delete a structure

  • To edit a structure, select it and click in the General tab.
  • To delete a structure, select it and click in the General tab.

  • To delete an agent, select them and click in the Human Resources tab.

Agent access rights

Each agent is given an access right that determines which modules and actions are available to them:

  • Top Manager — full access; can grant every right.
  • Human Resources Manager and Human Resources Assistant — staff and attendance management; can grant every right except Top Manager.
  • Finance and Accounting Manager and Finance and Accounting Assistant — Finance & Accounting module. The manager can grant the Ordinary, Finance Assistant and Finance Manager rights.
  • Material Resources Manager and Material Resources Assistant — the only ones who can add, edit and delete material and freely assign it (department, agent); review of material assignment. The manager can grant the Ordinary, Material Resources Assistant and Material Resources Manager rights.
  • Manager (head of department) — manages their department; can grant the Ordinary and Manager rights.
  • Ordinary — access to everyday features (activities, events, communication, leave and payslip self-service).
ℹ️ New: the Material Resources Assistant right lets an agent assist the Material Resources Manager, in particular to review material assignment.

🌴Leave

Leave management covers two uses: self-service, where each agent submits and tracks their own requests, and HR processing, grouped in the Human resources management module.

Open the Human resources management module

From the General tab, select the Human Resources tab at the bottom of the interface and click . The module opens in its own window, organized into three groups: Staff (Agent directory), Leave (Leave requests, Leave balances, Leave types) and Payroll (Payslips, Payslip template).

Submit a leave request (agent)

  1. From the main menu, open My leave requests. At the top of the screen, Days remaining shows the available balance.
  2. Click New request, enter the leave type and dates, then confirm.
  3. The request appears in the table (Type, Period, Days, Status, Submitted on, Decided on, Decision), where you can follow its progress.
  4. Filter by Fiscal year or Status, or search for a specific request. Select a row and click Open for details.
ℹ️ Note: A submitted request can still be withdrawn with Cancel request; a request that has already been decided can no longer be cancelled.

Approve or reject a request (HR manager)

  1. In Human resources management, open the Leave requests card.
  2. Filter by structure, agent, fiscal year or status if needed, then switch between the Awaiting decision and History tabs.
  3. Select a pending request (Agent, Type, Period, Days, Submitted on, Reason) and click Approve or Reject.
ℹ️ Important: Approving or rejecting is final and records your name on the decision. Agents submit their requests from their own screen; this module cannot be used to create one on their behalf.

View leave balances

The Leave balances card shows, for any agent, the leave counters automatically recalculated from requests that have already been approved.

Configure leave types

The Leave types card lets you set up the available leave categories (annual leave, sick leave, etc.) and their accrual rules.

🧾Payslips

Each agent views their own payslips on a self-service basis; generating them is reserved for HR and finance/accounting managers.

View your payslips (agent)

  1. From the main menu, open My payslips: the screen shows your name and the number of available payslips.
  2. Filter the list by year or status, then click Refresh.
  3. Select a payslip in the list (period, issue date, gross salary, total deductions, net pay, status): its preview appears in the right-hand panel.
  4. Use Print or Export PDF to keep a copy.

Generate a payslip (HR manager)

In Human resources management (Payroll group), the Payslips card — “Payslip calculation and printing” — opens the Payslip screen (“Payslip entry and preview”).

  1. Choose the team and then the agent concerned, as well as the payroll month and year — or reuse an existing payslip with Load from... — then enter the issue and payment dates.
  2. Under Earnings, enter the base salary; bonuses and other earnings items are added in the detailed list to obtain the gross salary.
  3. Under Deductions, itemize contributions and other deductions: their sum makes up the total deductions.
  4. The Results and employer cost section gives the net salary as well as the total cost to the employer.
  5. Once the entries have been checked, save the payslip; it can then be printed or exported to PDF.
ℹ️ Important: Once issued, a payslip permanently keeps its own figures — a payslip issued last year keeps the contribution rate in force at the time, even if the payroll template has changed since.

Configure the payroll template (HR manager)

The Payslip template card — “Configure earnings, deductions and employer charges” — defines the items offered when generating a new payslip (see the overview of the Human resources management module above).

IPRES contributions and income tax (automatic computation)

  1. In Payslip template, click Legal rubrics (IPRES, IR): the missing rubrics are created once and for all (IPRES general scheme and managers' scheme, employee and employer shares, and the income tax).
  2. On the record of each manager, tick Cadre (IPRES managers' scheme).
  • IPRES general scheme (every agent): 5.60% employee and 8.40% employer, on the gross capped at 432,000 FCFA a month.
  • IPRES managers' scheme (agents ticked "Cadre"): 2.40% employee and 3.60% employer, on the part of the gross between 432,000 and 1,296,000 FCFA.
  • Income tax: computed from the official withholding scale, after the 30% abatement (at most 75,000 FCFA a month) and according to the agent's fiscal parts (marital status and dependent children).
ℹ️ Note: The IPRES employee share lowers the net but not the income tax base: the 30% abatement already stands for retirement contributions (CGI, art. 168 b). A transport allowance (expense reimbursement) is entered as a net adjustment (+), not as an earning, so it stays out of the IPRES base. Rates remain editable in the template; a rate left empty takes the legal rate.

Settlement of an already validated payslip is handled in the Finance & Accounting module, via the Payroll settlement card.

⏱️Attendance (QR code clock-in)

Agents record their arrivals and departures by scanning, with their phone's camera, the QR code shown on a kiosk placed at the office entrance. No app needs to be installed: the scan opens a web page. The QR code changes every 30 seconds, and a clock-in is only accepted when the phone is connected to the office Wi-Fi, so a photo of the code or a clock-in from home is refused.

Clock in (agent)

  1. At the entrance, scan the kiosk's QR code with your phone's camera, then open the suggested link. The phone must be connected to the office Wi-Fi.
  2. On the first scan only, enter your xPertManager user name and password: your phone is then linked to your account. Only one phone can be linked to an agent.
  3. After that, scanning is enough: the page confirms the clock-in and shows the recorded time.
  • The clock-in type is automatic: the first scan of the day is an arrival, the next one a departure, and so on.
  • The recorded time is the server's time, not the phone's.
  • A second scan less than two minutes after the previous one is ignored.
ℹ️ Note: The link is kept by the phone's web browser. If you change phones, use private browsing or clear the browser's data, the clock-in will be refused: ask HR to reset your phone, then sign in again at the next scan.

View attendance

In the General tab, select the Human Resources tab at the bottom of the interface and click ⏱️. The Attendance (QR clock-in) window opens.

  1. Choose the period (From / To), then, if needed, a Structure and an Agent, and click Refresh.
  2. The Daily summary tab shows one line per agent and per day: arrival, departure, time present, number of clock-ins and remarks (departure not clocked, manual entry, refused attempts…).
  3. The All clock-ins tab lists each clock-in: time, type, status (recorded or refused), origin (QR code or manual), reason and IP address.
  4. Export CSV saves the displayed table to a file that opens in a spreadsheet.

Who can see what:

  • the HR manager, their assistant and the TOP Manager see the clock-ins of all agents;
  • a structure manager sees those of the agents of their structure and its sub-structures;
  • other agents see only their own clock-ins.

Manage attendance (HR manager, HR assistant, TOP Manager)

  • Manual entry…: records a forgotten clock-in (agent, date, time, type and a required reason). The entry is traced with your name.
  • Cancel the clock-in…: cancels the selected clock-in. It stays in the history, marked as cancelled.
  • Agent's phone…: shows the phone linked to the chosen agent (link date, last use) and lets you reset it, for example after a phone change or loss.
  • Entrance kiosk…: gives the address of the page to open in full screen on the tablet or PC placed at the entrance, connected to the office Wi-Fi. Keep this address confidential.
ℹ️ Notifications: Each clock-in updates the Attendance window in real time. Refused attempts (expired code, phone outside the office Wi-Fi, unrecognized phone) appear in the HR managers' notification center.
ℹ️ For the administrator: The clock-in service is built into the xPertManager server. In production, the clock-in page must be served over HTTPS (domain name and certificate), because agents enter their password there when linking their phone.

📁Creating and managing projects or activities

Create a project or activity

  1. In the General tab, select the structure in which the project or activity is to be created.
  2. In the Activities tab, select the parent project or activity. If there is none, select the type (professional or personal).
  3. Click .
  4. In the dialog box, fill in the required data. The Milestones field describes the key steps of the activity. If activity types have not been defined, they must be set up by editing the parent structure.
  5. Click Insert.
  6. You can continue creating other projects or close the dialog box.
ℹ️ Note: Co-authors can be defined at creation time; they will be able to edit the project or create sub-activities.

Edit / Delete

  • To edit a project, select it and click in the Activities tab.
  • To delete a project, select it and click in the Activities tab.

Manage a project or activity

  • Add involved agents: Click to choose the participating staff.
  • Attach a document: Click to attach a file to the project.
  • Publish a project: Click to make it available to collaborators.
  • Mark as completed: Click once finished.
  • Define dependencies: Use the “Previous activities” option.
  • Assign resources: Use the “Assign resources” option to allocate material or human resources.
  • Visualization: Display as a Gantt or MPM chart, animate progress in real time with the Animate checkbox (activities are automatically colored according to their progress: normal, critical, completed, not yet started) and export the charts.

🎥Organizing a video conference

Instant video conference

Scheduled video conference

🔧Managing material resources

Add a material resource

ℹ️ Rights: only the Material Resources Manager and the Material Resources Assistant can add, edit, delete and assign a material resource. A head of department can only reassign (department and agent) the material of their department and its sub-departments. For other agents, including the Top Manager, the Material Resources tab is view-only.
  1. Select the General tab.
  2. Select the structure to which the material resource will be assigned.
  3. Select the Material Resources tab at the bottom of the interface.
  4. Click the button.
  5. In the dialog box, choose the category (or add one), then enter the designation, the reference (which must be unique) and the acquisition date.
  6. In Assign to, choose the department the material is assigned to.
  7. In Assign to agent, optionally choose the agent of that department who uses the material, or leave — Nobody — if the material is assigned to the department only. The list of agents is updated whenever the department changes.
  8. Click Add.
  9. You can continue adding other resources or close the dialog box.
ℹ️ Note: The material resources table shows the Reference, designation, category, department and assigned agent of each resource. The CSV export also includes the assigned agent.

Edit / Delete a material resource

  • To edit, select the resource in the Material Resources tab and click . The reference can no longer be changed after creation. A head of department can only reassign (department and agent) the material of their department and its sub-departments.
  • To delete, select the resource and click .

Review material assignment (Material Resources Manager / assistant)

The Material assignment window shows which material is assigned to a department and to its agents.

  1. In the Material Resources tab, click the Material assignment button.
  2. Choose the Department, then in the Agent list:
    • — All material (department and sub-departments) —: all the material of the department and its sub-departments, whoever it is assigned to; the Department and Assigned agent columns are then shown;
    • — Nobody (not assigned to an agent) —: the department's material that is not assigned to any agent;
    • an agent: the material assigned to that agent.
  3. Above the table, the Category list lets you display all categories or a single category of material.
  4. The table shows the Reference, Designation and Acquisition date; the number of items is shown below the lists. A scroll bar appears when the list is long.
ℹ️ Note: This button is only active for the Material Resources Manager and the Material Resources Assistant. The window is read-only: changes are made with .

Schedule a material resource

💰Finance & Accounting

The Finance and accounting hub, opened from the Finance/Accounting tab, groups the modules into three families: Sales and purchasing (Customers, Suppliers, Orders, Invoices), Catalog and stock (Products, Stock) and Finance and payroll (Treasury, Bank reconciliation, Payroll settlement). Each card opens its module in its own window.

Manage customers and suppliers

  1. Open the Customers card (“Customer records and contacts”) or the Suppliers card (“Supplier records and contacts”).
  2. Click New customer (or New supplier) and enter the code, the name or company name, the customer type where applicable, the phone number, email, address and active status.
  3. Click Save.
  • Search or filter by type, or tick Active only.
  • Select an existing record to edit it or Delete it; Clear empties the form.

Track orders and invoices

The Orders card (“Sales and purchase orders”) lists the orders placed with customers and suppliers:

  • Create a New sales order or a New purchase order, and filter by type or status.
  • Select an order to Open, Confirm, Mark as delivered or Cancel it.
  • Once the order is confirmed, Generate invoice creates the corresponding invoice.

The Invoices card (“Issuing, payments, cancellation”) then lets you track each invoice:

  • The invoice details (number, type, linked order, invoice and due dates, status) and its amounts (excl. tax, VAT, incl. tax, paid, balance due) are shown in the right-hand panel.
  • Enter a Payment amount and click Record payment; an invoice can also be cancelled with Cancel invoice.
  • Use Export to PDF or Print to get the document.

Manage the product catalog and stock

  1. Open the Products card (“Catalog, prices, alert thresholds”) and click New product.
  2. Enter the purchase and selling prices excluding tax, as well as the tax rate: the selling price including tax and the margin are calculated automatically.
  3. Enter the quantity in stock and the alert threshold; a low stock badge appears automatically when the threshold is reached.
  4. Click Save.

The log of stock receipts and issues is available in the Stock card (“Movement and adjustment log”).

Record an expense or income

  1. Open the Treasury card and click New entry.
  2. Choose the type (expense or income), a category, a description, an amount and, if needed, a note.
  3. Confirm: the entry appears in the table and is immediately reflected in the indicators and charts.

Expense categories: rent, electricity, water, telecoms, supplies, transport, insurance, maintenance, taxes and duties, salaries, purchases, other. Income categories: sales, grants, interest, refunds, other.

  • An existing entry can be edited or deleted from the table.
  • Generate report exports the displayed period.

Monitor treasury

  1. Choose the period to analyze (start month and year, end month and year), then click Refresh.
  2. The key indicators — Total income, Total expenses, Balance, Margin rate — are updated at the top of the screen.
  3. The Monthly treasury trend chart traces income and expenses month by month; the Expense breakdown and Income breakdown pie charts break each down by category, with a Breakdown by category table (Category, Type, Amount) below.

Reconcile the bank statement (TOP Manager / finance manager / assistant)

The Bank reconciliation card compares, line by line, the statement sent by the bank with the treasury entries already recorded in the application, to check that they match.

  1. Open the Bank reconciliation card, choose the period to process (start date, end date), then click Refresh.
  2. The left-hand table shows the bank statement lines (date, description, amount, status); the right-hand table shows the treasury entries for the same period.
  3. If a statement line does not yet exist in the application, click Add and enter its date, description and amount.
  4. Select a statement line and its matching treasury entry, then click Reconcile: the line changes to Reconciled status.
  5. If a match was made in error, select the reconciled line and click Undo reconciliation.
ℹ️ Note: The number of reconciled lines, the number of unreconciled lines and the difference between the statement total and the entries total are shown at the bottom of the screen and are updated with each reconciliation.

Access to this module is restricted to the TOP Manager, the finance manager and their assistant.

Settle a payslip (finance/accounting manager)

The Payroll settlement card (“Validated payslips awaiting payment”) lists the payslips already validated by the HR manager and lets you record their settlement (payment method, status, remarks) — see also the Payslips section.

💬Audio calls, text messages and instant messaging

ℹ️ Different networks: audio calls, chat and messages work even when agents are not on the same network (company network, home Wi-Fi, mobile hotspot): traffic goes through the server when a direct connection is not possible.

Make an audio call

  1. Select the General tab.
  2. Select the Human Resources tab at the bottom of the interface.
  3. Select the agent you want to call (they must be online).
  4. Click the button.

Send a text message

  1. Select the General tab, then the Human Resources tab at the bottom of the interface.
  2. Select the recipient agent (even if they are offline) and click the button. The window opens in To mode (writing).
  3. Add or remove recipients with (tick the agents in the list; ● marks a connected agent), or load a distribution group with .
  4. Enter the Subject and the text of the message. Tick High priority if the message should pop up as a sticky note for the recipient as soon as it arrives (or when they log in).
  5. If needed, attach a document with (16 MB maximum), then click Send. A report lists the recipients reached.
ℹ️ Attachment: The recipient downloads the document from the messages window. It is deleted from the server as soon as they have saved it, and in any case 48 hours after sending.

Read received and sent messages

In the messages window, the list at the top left offers three modes: To (writing), From (received messages) and Sent. In From and Sent modes, the window is split in two:

  • On the left, one line per contact (the sender in From mode, the recipient in Sent mode): their name, the date and a preview of the last message, and the number of messages. While unread messages remain, the name is in bold and a badge shows the number of new messages.
  • On the right, all the messages exchanged with that contact, from oldest to newest: date, subject, text and any attachment.
  • The field at the top summarizes the box: number of messages and of unread messages.

In From mode:

  • opening a conversation marks its messages as read;
  • Reply goes back to writing, with the sender as recipient and "RE:" before the subject;
  • the message selected on the right can be turned into a sticky note , deleted or, if it has an attachment, downloaded with .

In Sent mode, each message shows whether it was ✓ Read or Not read by its recipient, and the state of its attachment (not downloaded yet, or no longer on the server).

ℹ️ Note: A sent message and the received message are one and the same record. For this reason, a sent message cannot be deleted from Sent mode, and it disappears from that mode if its recipient deletes or archives it.

Start an instant messaging chat

  1. Select the General tab.
  2. Select the Human Resources tab at the bottom of the interface.
  3. Select the agent you want to chat with (they must be online).
  4. Click the button.

🔔Notification center

The bell 🔔, at the top right of the General tab, keeps the history of your communications: audio calls, chats, text messages and video conferences, received or placed, including missed, declined or unanswered calls. HR managers also receive refused clock-in attempts there. A red badge on the bell shows the number of unread notifications.

  1. Click the bell to open the Communication notifications window.
  2. The table shows the date, type, direction (received or sent), contact, status (answered, missed, declined, line busy, unreachable…) and details of each notification. Filter by type if needed.
  3. Mark all as read resets the bell's counter.
  • Keep protects the selected notifications; Don't keep removes that protection.
  • Delete removes the selected notifications; Delete not kept removes all the others.
ℹ️ Note: Notifications are stored on your computer, for your account only. Beyond 1,000 notifications, the oldest ones that are not kept are deleted automatically.

🗳️Electronic voting

Electronic voting is built into an event. It can be scheduled or held instantly. To organize an electronic vote:

  1. Launch the associated event.
  2. Click the button.
  3. Enter the vote title in the dialog box that appears and click OK.
  4. Create and add the voting options.
  5. Specify the closing time of the vote.
  6. Click Start to open the vote. The options will appear in all participants' interfaces so that they can vote.
  7. At the end of the vote, click Stop. The results will appear in the interfaces of all participants invited to the vote — including those who did not vote — and for the organizer. The displayed list of voters only includes the people who actually voted.

✨Miscellaneous: Eisenhower matrix, sticky notes, distribution groups

Eisenhower matrix

The Eisenhower matrix is built into the dashboard. It gives the user a clear view of:

  • Important and urgent tasks — do immediately
  • Important but not urgent tasks — schedule
  • Not important but urgent tasks — delegate
  • Not important and not urgent tasks — can be ignored

Activities or events created or received are automatically placed in the matrix — in the Important and urgent tasks area if the deadline is close, otherwise in Important but not urgent tasks. The user can reassign priorities from the Activities and Events tabs by selecting an item and clicking .

Create sticky notes

  1. In the General tab, click the button and choose Edit....
  2. In the dialog box, click Add to create a sticky note.
  3. You can continue creating other notes or close the dialog box.

Display sticky notes

  • Click the button and choose Show/Hide.
  • The notes will appear and scroll one after another.
  • To close the window, click in the window, or click and then Show/Hide.

Create distribution groups

  1. Select the General tab.
  2. Select the Human Resources tab at the bottom of the interface.
  3. Click the button.
  4. In the dialog box, enter the group name and add members.
  5. Save the group to a file for future use.
